## PACT: Purpose, Assumptions, Core flow, Tradeoffs

Purpose: Identify the user, main action, expected result and exclusions. Move on when you can what what you are building in 1 or 2 sentences.

Assumptions: Confirm constrains that should change the design. State reasonable assumptions for missing details -> Move on when you have enough information to attempt the main flow.

Core flow: Draw the simplest design. Trace one request through its API, logic, data and response. Move on when you can explain how the flow works from start to finish.

Tradeoffs: Challenge one choice with a failure, concurrent requests, more traffic, its fine to take a step back. Move on when you can explain why the change is worth its cost. Ot why you would keep the simpler version.
