# The assessment — actual format

From the official interview guide, received Mon 2026-08-31. **This supersedes the rough
four-part description in CLAUDE.md.** Everything runs in the **CodeSignal** application.

| Round | Length | Shape |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Behavioral** | 60 min | "Tell me about a time when…" · STAR method |
| **Case — Tech Architectural** | 60 min | A business problem, solved *with* the interviewer, in 3 parts |
| **Technical — hands-on** | 60 min | Terraform / CloudFormation, live, in CodeSignal |
| **Technical — concept-focused** | 60 min | Q&A across 9 named areas |

## Behavioral — STAR

**Situation / Task** — a specific event, not a general description. Enough detail that the
role is clear.
**Action** — what **you** did. Say "I", not "we". Real actions, including missteps.
**Result** — what happened, what it accomplished, **and what you learned**.

Skills they name: communication · influencing others · collaboration · driving a project
start to finish.

Sample prompts given:
- Used persuasion to convince someone to see things your way.
- Used good judgement and logic to solve a problem.
- Went above and beyond to get a job done.
- Had too many things to do and had to prioritise.
- Anticipated a problem and built a preventive measure.

Have **2 examples per skill**. Pausing to think before answering is explicitly allowed.

## Case — three parts

1. **Framing** — a business situation plus background data. Make key observations, name the
   issues to consider.
2. **Drivers** — more specific questions. New data arrives; draw conclusions from it and defend
   them under follow-ups.
3. **Recommendation** — asked throughout, and once at the end. **Changing your recommendation as
   data arrives is expected, not penalised.**

## Hands-on technical — what they grade

- Navigate, understand and **improve** cloud configuration.
- **Receive and adapt to feedback** — recognising and fixing a mistake is explicitly graded.
- Explain the reasoning behind the solution.

## Concept-focused — the 9 areas

1. Python / coding / scripting
2. Security and permissions
3. AWS cloud services and solutions architecture
4. CI/CD — Jenkins or similar
5. Containers — Docker, Kubernetes
6. IaC — CloudFormation, **Terraform, Ansible**
7. Monitoring, logging, troubleshooting, alerting
8. Networking
9. Scalability and reliability

## What this changed in the plan

- Behavioral moved out of micro-slots into a real 45-minute writing block. It is a quarter of
  the day and needs no technical knowledge.
- **Ansible**, **Jenkins** and **Python/boto3** added — none appeared in the original brief.
- Kubernetes and CloudFormation depth reduced to pay for it.
- Open codesignal.com once before Thursday. Logistics, not study.
