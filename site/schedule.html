17:00–18:00 is a window, not a quota. The work is 3 reps, which takes 35–45 minutes. If you finish at 17:40, you stop. Do not fill the hour — filling it is how 9 hours becomes 12 and the plan dies.

The three levels of a day

Say this to yourself when you feel behind.

Floor35 min

3 reps, nothing else. On a rough night this is a win. The streak survives.

Standard~75 min

3 reps plus the evening block.

Full~2 h

Adds the new DSA problem. 3 times a week, Mon–Wed — energy falls across the week.

The new DSA problem is optional by design. A night at Floor is not a miss.

System design — the weekly cycle

One design a week. A 40-minute slot cannot hold an attempt and a review, so it is split.

  1. Mon — Attempt. Timed, blind, no reading. Boxes and arrows. Numbers out loud.→ attempt.md + sketch
  2. Wed — Check. Compare against the reference. Run the 3×3 sweep. Write every question you could not answer.→ gaps.md
  3. Fri — Close one gap. Take a single item from gaps.md and learn it properly, with a diagram.→ a note in concepts/

Every third Friday, re-draw an old design blind instead. See REVIEW-QUEUE.md.

LLD — the weekly cycle

One pattern a week. Every ~4 weeks, one full problem combining several.

  1. Tue — Study one pattern. What problem it solves, and what it costs.
  2. Thu — Sketch Sunday's build: the class list and the interfaces, on paper.
  3. Sun — Build it in Python, by hand, 1.5–2 hours.

Build the wrong version too, so you can feel why the pattern exists. The goal is taste, not a finished app.

The rules

  1. Practice means producing an artifact. Reading an article or watching a video is not practice. A bad one-page design beats a well-understood article.
  2. Saturday is off, and Friday ends early. Rest pays for the other six days. It is not what is left over.
  3. Do not add more. Hitting Full five nights a week is overdrawing, not dedication.
  4. Concepts are pulled, not pushed. Never study a topic you have not already needed. The gap list is the curriculum.
  5. Never open a reference design before your attempt exists. The gap between the two is the entire lesson.
